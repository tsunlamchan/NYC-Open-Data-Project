About Us

In today's world almost everyone depends on a cellular device, laptop, or tablet. With that comes the need for reliable WIFI access. Our project focuses on helping people locate nearby WIFI hotspots before leaving home, making it easier to stay connected wherever they go.

Why Public WIFI Matters

Public WIFI hotspots help students, workers, tourists, and families stay connected when mobile data is unavailable or expensive.

Libraries, parks, restaurants, and transportation hubs often provide free internet access that supports communication, learning, and navigation.

About Us

In today's world, almost everyone depends on a cell phone, laptop, or tablet. Because of this, having reliable WIFI access is more important than ever. Our project focuses on helping people find nearby WIFI hotspots before they leave home, making it easier to stay connected wherever they go.

Internet access is used every day for school, work, communication, and entertainment. Students may need WIFI to complete assignments, while others use it to check directions, send messages, watch videos, or browse the web. Public WIFI hotspots can be found in places like parks, libraries, restaurants, and transportation stations, but many people do not know where these locations are or how to find them quickly.

By exploring hotspot locations, maps, and statistics, users can learn where internet access is available and which areas have the most hotspots. Our goal is to make finding WIFI simple and convenient while helping people stay connected in today's digital world.
